AutoSlides model notices
========================

These model files are separate works that AutoSlides loads as data at run
time, either from the app bundle or as a separate download. Each keeps its
own licence; the AutoSlides licence (Apache-2.0) does not apply to them,
and its terms do not restrict the rights those licences grant.

slide-detect-v1.onnx
  Slide canvas detector (auto-crop). A YOLOv8 model fine-tuned with
  Ultralytics by the AutoSlides author in a separate project.
  Licence: GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 (AGPL-3.0.txt).
  Source (training code and instructions): https://github.com/bit-admin/slide-crop
  You may use your own model instead: Settings > Image Processing >
  Auto Crop > Select Custom Model.

slide-classifier-v1.onnx
  Slide / not-slide classifier (post-processing). A MobileNetV4 model
  trained by the AutoSlides author in a separate project.
  Licence: MIT (slide-classifier-MIT.txt).
  Source: https://github.com/bit-admin/slide-classifier
