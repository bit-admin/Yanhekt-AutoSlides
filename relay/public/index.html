What this means?

This relay runs on Cloudflare, and Cloudflare has no data centre in mainland China. Traffic from campus therefore always leaves the country to reach the nearest Cloudflare city.

Hong Kong (HKG) is usually the shortest hop to the video origin in China. Singapore (SIN), Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Taipei, and Macau are also nearby and typically play well.

On campus you will most often land in Hong Kong or Singapore. Cloudflare can still send you farther — Los Angeles (LAX) and San Jose (SJC) show up sometimes — and that longer path is slower.

This service allows campus networks on the list above. Other networks may be challenged or blocked by Cloudflare.

Routing ruc.edu.kg through a Hong Kong proxy can shorten a detour, but off-campus you still have to pass a Cloudflare challenge in the browser first. Dedicated players (VLC, ffmpeg, and many HLS apps) cannot complete that challenge, so they will fail even after this page loads.

The login token is embedded in the generated URL (so HLS players can fetch every segment). Treat the URL as a secret — anyone with it can stream as you until the token expires. Recorded videos only.
